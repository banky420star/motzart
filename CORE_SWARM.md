# Core Swarm Contract — Mozart 2.1.1

The organism is not a node graph with glow effects. It is the live physical representation of project intelligence and execution.

## Liquid motion language

1. **Breathing core** — the Mozart body continuously deforms asymmetrically; it is never a perfect circle.
2. **Core compression + wave** — active work increases pressure and compresses the body before release.
3. **Bulge → neck → pinch-off → travel** — a worker begins as a local membrane bulge, narrows through a neck, detaches, then stretches under travel.
4. **Return + absorption** — completed work moves inward, shrinks, and rejoins the core.
5. **Budding / new cell** — persistent project capabilities grow as irregular membrane organs from real agents/jobs/files.
6. **Amber ring** — assignment, successful return, and growth events produce an amber expansion ring.
7. **Red shock ring** — failure/rejection/blocking produces a red shock response.
8. **Small diamond seed** — moving work cells retain the bright diamond seed.
9. **Brighter / heavier tendon** — workload increases tendon thickness, brightness, and local pressure bulges.

## Physics

**Elastic drag + settle** is mandatory. Organs lag their structural targets with spring/damping behavior, stretch in the direction of tension, and settle rather than snapping into position.

## Morphology

- No perfect circular organs.
- 3–7 irregular lobes per living body.
- Soft double membrane with internal flow.
- Active cells stretch and flatten under tension.
- Dense connected topology; subsystems should feel grown from the same body.
- QA/review is transient unless it creates durable project structure.

## Interaction

Core, tendons, organs, moving work cells and file buds are selectable. Hover labels remain contextual and hidden at rest so the organism dominates the interface.
